PCA and Random Forest

Supervised Learning: RF 
- knows the categories we are trying to predict at outset
- maximize variance between groups 
- classifier (using subset of data to train, then predict based on the training groups with data it hasn't seen yet) 
- can use one decision tree if you want to partition data (bootstrapping random permutions of decision trees to prevent overfitting)
- linear discriminant analysis (uses a structure like PCA to make linear models of variables)



Unsupervised Learning: PCA (NMDS - non parametric version)
- doesn't know diff between categories we want to predict
- trying to understand variance in model and partition vars into diff segments called PCs 
- dimensionality reduction (at heart)
- PCA assumes normal distribution (a must)
